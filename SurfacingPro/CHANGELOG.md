# SurfacingPro — CHANGELOG

## 0.1.0 - Experimental (first public release)

- **Model in the level**: primitives (box, cylinder) cut with a plane; bezier patches (patch, cone, tube, bevel, endcap) edited as control grids - extrude, loop, push, steps, bevel, subdivide, weld, join, bridge, separate, duplicate, delete, solidify, smooth, inflate, flatten, random, flip, face outward, apply. Continue joins two borders as one surface, and offers it while you move.
- **Select** with 1 2 3 4, box, lasso and brush, all / none / invert / same / every other, Alt for a whole line, through surfaces in wireframe.
- **Move** with G R S, axis locks and typed numbers, or the gizmo; soft selection on the wheel; snapping to the point under the cursor, primitive corners, the surface under the cursor, and anything within reach.
- **Surface** by projection: Flow, Even, Align, Project, Fit, Density, Freeze; the Materials window; middle-click to copy a surface; Replace across the level; the texel readout; the UV window with G R S and exact figures.
- **Layers** 0-3 and vertex-colour painting with a Paint window.
- **Prefabs and levels** from the selection, opened where they stand or alone, placed from the Prefabs window, unpacked or made unique; prefabs inside prefabs.
- **No bake step**: Game view, Play, Save and keeping a level turn the geometry into merged Nanite static meshes; any edit brings it back. Flags per piece.
- **A quiet interface**: a bar of ten, the shelf, family menus with the key at the right, Q favourites, X search, a frame that pops on use with the verb's knobs and options, a one-line foot with the keys of the moment, two key schemes, every key rebindable, Edit UI to arrange the chips.
